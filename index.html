FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Argus really free?

Yes. Every feature is free for everyone. Argus is supported by ads from Google, which never appear during setup, on the security log, on the app lock or on the block screen. You can watch one short video to go ad-free for 6 hours.

Does Argus upload my data?

No. Your activity data is processed and stored only on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google's ad service needs to show ads, such as your advertising ID. See the privacy policy.

How does Argus know about wrong PIN attempts?

You can let Argus be told about failed unlocks by turning on its device admin access. It uses this only to be told about wrong attempts. It can't lock, wipe or change your phone.

Why does Argus need usage access?

Android already records when your phone is unlocked and which apps you use. Usage access lets Argus read those records to count unlocks and screen time. It can't see what you do inside apps.

Can I use Argus to monitor someone else?

No. Argus is made for the person who owns the phone. It shows an ongoing notification while it's recording and can't be hidden.

Does it drain my battery?

Argus mostly reads records Android already keeps, so it typically uses under 2% of your battery a day.

Which phones are supported?

Android 10 and later. Some phones (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Samsung, Huawei and others) stop apps in the background, so Argus shows you the exact steps for your phone during setup.

How do I delete my data?

Open Argus → Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling Argus also deletes everything it recorded.

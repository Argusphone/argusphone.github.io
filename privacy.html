Argus Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · App: Argus (app.argus.guard)

The short version

Everything Argus records about your phone stays on your phone. Argus has no account, no server and no cloud. We, the developer, never receive your activity data. The only data that leaves your phone goes to Google's ad service, which shows the ads that keep Argus free.

What Argus records on your device

To provide its features, Argus reads and stores the following only on your device:

  • When your phone is unlocked, turned on and off, and which apps you open and for how long (from Android's usage records)
  • Failed unlock attempts (the time only; never your PIN, pattern or password)
  • An intruder photo from the front camera on a failed attempt, only if you turn this on
  • A rough place name, or coordinates rounded to about 1 km, on a failed attempt, only if you turn this on
  • Mobile and Wi-Fi data used per app, storage per app, and battery level, temperature and charging history
  • The list of installed apps and which apps hold special access, for the security check-up
  • How many notifications each app posts (counts only; never their content)
  • Your app limits, which apps you've locked, and your settings
  • Your Argus PIN, only if you set one, stored as a salted hash, never the PIN itself

This data is kept in an encrypted database. Its key is protected by your phone's hardware-backed Android Keystore. Intruder photos are encrypted the same way. Argus never reads your messages, photos, files, passwords, what you type or the websites you visit.

How long it's kept, and your control

  • Activity is kept for the history period you choose in Settings, then deleted automatically. At most 100 intruder photos are kept.
  • You can export your own data as CSV or PDF at any time. The file is created on your device, and you choose where to share it.
  • Settings → Delete all data erases everything Argus has recorded. Uninstalling Argus also deletes it.
  • Every permission can be turned off at any time in Android Settings.

Advertising

Argus is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, interactions with ads, and diagnostic information. This data is sent to Google and handled under Google's privacy policy. None of your Argus activity data is shared with Google or anyone else.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), Argus asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Optional: Shizuku

If you choose to use Advanced mode, Argus uses the Shizuku app on your device to list running services, processes and scheduled jobs. Those results stay on your device.

Children

Argus is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Your rights

Because we don't receive your Argus activity data, there is nothing about your use of Argus for us to access, correct or delete. Your on-device data is fully under your control, as described above. For data collected by Google for ads, see Google's privacy policy.

Changes and contact
